Method. Every day of 2026 was reconstructed from the trip database behind travel.aeroverra.com: flight departure and arrival dates, hotel check-in through check-out, cruise embark, port calls and disembark. Days with no booking either side are only filled in when the bookings before and after agree on the country. Sea days belong to no country and are counted on their own. Territories are counted as their own entry, same as any country. Map routes are great circles between real booked ports and airports, not recorded tracks.
