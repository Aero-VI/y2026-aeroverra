Method. Every figure on this page is derived from the trip database behind travel.aeroverra.com: flight confirmations, hotel bookings and cruise manifests, 2005 to now. Days are assigned by booking (flight departure and arrival dates, hotel check-in through check-out, cruise embark, port calls and disembark); an unbooked day is only filled when the bookings either side agree on the country. Sea days belong to no country and are counted on their own. Distances are great circles between real coordinates, so they are minimums, not flown tracks. Air time comes from the booked schedule on 38 of the 39 flights; one Vienna to Malta ticket has no times on the confirmation. Territories are counted as their own entry. Generated 1 November 2026.
